      About My Shows

      Small jazz, creative and experimental shows around Los Angeles, pulled straight from venue and label websites a few times a day.

      On a keyboard: left and right change the day, up and down move between that day's shows, and Enter saves the one you're looking at.

      On a phone: swipe sideways to change the day, swipe up and down to move between shows, and tap Save.

      Everything you save stays in this browser only.
